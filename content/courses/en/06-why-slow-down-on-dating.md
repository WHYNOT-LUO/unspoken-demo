## Liking someone is normal

As you grow up, it is natural to start having feelings for someone, to want to be close to them, and to want them to care about you. There is nothing shameful or wrong about it.

This lesson is not here to tell you "you're not allowed to like anyone". It's here to think through with you why many adults suggest you take dating **a bit more slowly**.

## What adults worry about

- **Emotions can swing a lot**: the happiness and the sadness in a relationship can both be very intense, and can affect your sleep, your studies and your friendships.
- **You're still learning how relationships work**: how to say what you need, how to argue and make up, how to end things well. These skills take time to learn.
- **It's easy to be pushed into things you're not ready for**: sometimes people force themselves to do things so they won't lose the other person.
- **Some "relationships" are really a search for warmth**: if you rarely feel cared for at home, you may long for someone else's attention, and that can make it easier to get hurt.

## Ask yourself first

If you like someone, or you are already in a relationship, try asking yourself:

- Do I like this person, or the feeling that "someone cares about me"?
- In this relationship, do I feel relaxed and respected?
- Have I given up things I used to enjoy, or friends, because of this person?

## When you really want someone to care about you

Wanting to be cared about is a need everyone has. Besides dating, you can also find warmth here:

- chatting with friends, teachers or family members you trust;
- joining activities you enjoy and meeting people who share your interests;
- writing down your feelings and taking good care of yourself.

Be aware that some games, live streams and paid companionship services get people to spend a lot of money in exchange for the feeling of being cared about. If you notice you are spending more and more on them and can't stop, talk to an adult you trust.

## Reflection

- What does "being cared about" feel like to you? When did you last feel it?
- Apart from dating, where else can you find support and warmth?
